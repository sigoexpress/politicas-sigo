Support and contact

Here you will find who to write to depending on what you need, and what information to include so we can help you quickly.

Reporting a problem

So that we can reproduce the error, tell us:

  1. What you were doing when it happened.
  2. What you expected to happen and what happened instead.
  3. Your phone model and Android version.
  4. Your Sigo App version (Settings → About).
  5. A screenshot or short video, if you can.
Never share your password

The Sigo App team will never ask for your password, your OTP or a verification code — not by email, not by chat, not by phone. If someone does, it is an attempted fraud: do not reply and let us know at seguridad@sigoexpress.app.

Reporting a user or content

If someone is harassing you, impersonating an identity, sending spam or sharing illegal content, you can report them:

  1. From the conversation or profile, tap the ⋮ menu and choose Report.
  2. Choose the reason and, if you can, attach a screenshot.
  3. If you prefer to do it outside the app, write to abuso@sigoexpress.app with the username and what happened.

You can also block any user from their profile. Once blocked, they can no longer message you or see your information.

We review every report. Depending on severity, measures range from a warning to permanent suspension of the account, in accordance with section 9 of the Terms. If the content may constitute a criminal offence, we report it to the competent authorities.

Reporting a vulnerability

If you have found a security flaw, write to us at seguridad@sigoexpress.app with the steps to reproduce it. We ask that you:

  • Do not access, modify or delete other people's data.
  • Do not disclose the flaw publicly until we have fixed it.
  • Give us a reasonable period to resolve it.

In return, we commit to replying within 72 hours, keeping you updated on progress, and publicly crediting your contribution if you would like that.

Frequently asked questions

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot your password? and follow the steps. We will send a code to the email or phone you registered with.

Can Sigo App read my messages?

Your messages are stored encrypted and never in plain text, but we manage the keys, so technically it is possible to decrypt them. We only do so on a valid judicial request or to investigate a reported abuse. Calls and Walkie-Talkie do have end-to-end encryption. The detail is in section 3.4 of the Privacy Policy.

I want a copy of my data

Request it by writing to privacidad@sigoexpress.app. We deliver it in JSON format within 30 days.

I want to delete my account

Follow the steps on the account deletion page.
