Delete your account and your data

You can request deletion of your account whenever you want, from the app or from here. Below we explain precisely what is deleted, what is anonymised and what is kept.

Option 1 — From the app

In Sigo App, the exact path is:

  1. Open Sigo App and sign in.
  2. In the bottom bar, tap the Discover tab.
  3. Tap the Me button.
  4. At the very bottom, below Log Out, tap Delete account.
  5. Read what happens to your data, type DELETE and confirm with your password.

This is the fastest route: the request is registered straight away. We ask for your password as confirmation, so that nobody can delete your account with your unlocked phone in their hand.

Option 2 — By email, if you do not have the app

Write to us at privacidad@sigoexpress.app from the email address you registered with, and include:

  • Subject: Account deletion request
  • The email address or phone number you registered with, so we can identify your account
  • What you want deleted: the whole account, only the linked email account, or only the Täbu assistant history

We confirm receipt and the outcome within 30 calendar days.

Do not include your password

We will never ask for your password by email, and we do not need it: the email address or phone number from your registration is enough to identify your account. If someone asks for it on behalf of Sigo App, it is an attempted fraud.

What is deleted, anonymised and kept

Not everything is treated the same way, and we would rather say so before you ask.

Deleted entirely Anonymised Kept
Your conversations with the Täbu assistant and all their messages Your first and last names, which are replaced by "Deleted account" The messages and files you sent to other people, which remain in those recipients' conversations
Linked email accounts, synchronised email and their access tokens Your identity document number Call and Walkie-Talkie history, with its metadata
Your contact list within Sigo Your username, email address and phone number Data we must retain by legal obligation
Your profile picture, including the cloud copy Your postal address and date of birth —
Your sessions, pending verification codes and your device's notification identifier Your password, your Google and Facebook identifiers, and your QR code —
Why your messages are not deleted

The messages you sent live inside conversations shared with other people, just like an email that is already in the recipient's inbox. Deleting them would leave those conversations broken for them. What does disappear is everything that identifies you: from the final deletion onwards, where your name used to be, other people see "Deleted account".

Timing

WhenWhat happens
Immediately Your account is deactivated. We close your sessions, you stop receiving notifications and you can no longer sign in. We disconnect your linked email accounts.
For 30 days Recovery period: you can still change your mind. The people you were talking to still see your name in those conversations.
After 30 days Final deletion — automatic and irreversible. Your name is replaced by "Deleted account".
Up to 30 days more The data is overwritten in the encrypted backups.

If you sent your request by email, we confirm receipt and the outcome within 30 calendar days.

Recovering your account

You have 30 days to change your mind. Write to us at privacidad@sigoexpress.app from the email or phone number you registered with and we will reactivate the account with your history intact.

After 30 days this is no longer possible: the final deletion cannot be reversed.

Before you delete: download your data

Deletion is permanent and irreversible. If you want to keep a copy of your conversations and files, first request a data export by writing to us at privacidad@sigoexpress.app. We deliver it in JSON format within 30 days.

Just want to disconnect your email?

You do not need to delete your account. From the mail inbox, using the "Disconnect" button, we remove the tokens and the synchronised email, and your Sigo account keeps working normally. You can also revoke access from your Google account settings.

More information

The full detail of what data we process and for how long is in our Privacy Policy, in particular sections 7 (retention) and 11 (account deletion).
