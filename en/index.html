1. Who we are and the scope of this Policy

In this Privacy Policy we explain how Sigo App ("Sigo App", "Sigo" or "we") processes the personal data of people who use the application and its associated services (the "Services").

We are the data controller for that data. Our identification and contact details are in section 16.

For the purposes of this Policy, "you" or "the user" means the person using the Services. This Policy forms an integral part of the Sigo App Terms and Conditions and must be read together with them. By creating an account you confirm that you have read both documents.

1.1 Our principles

  • We collect only the data needed to run a secure, working communication platform.
  • We do not sell your personal data. We do not share it with third parties for advertising purposes or to build commercial profiles.
  • We do not use the content of your messages, calls or emails to train artificial intelligence models, neither our own nor third parties'.
  • We encrypt the content of your communications both in transit and at rest.
  • When a feature can run on your device instead of on our servers, we run it on your device.

1.2 Minimum age

Sigo App is intended exclusively for people aged 16 or over. This requirement applies to all accounts, including business accounts. This check is based on the date of birth you declare when registering: we do not currently carry out independent age verification. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16. If we detect, or are notified, that an account belongs to someone under 16, we will suspend it and delete its data. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a minor in your care has given us data, write to us at the address in section 16.

3. What data we collect

3.1 Data you give us when you sign up

Required

  • First and last names
  • National identity document number
  • Date of birth
  • Country of residence
  • Phone number and/or email address
  • Password (stored only as a salted hash; never in readable form)
Why we ask for your identity document

The document number is requested to verify that each account is unique and to prevent identity fraud and the mass creation of fraudulent accounts. It is not shared with other users and is not shown on your public profile, and it is stored with restricted access, limited to staff who need to consult it.

Optional

  • Profile photo
  • Gender
  • Postal address
  • Bio and website
  • Company details (legal name, address, phone, website), if you open a business account

3.2 Signing up through external providers

If you sign up with Google or Facebook, we receive your user identifier, name, email address and profile picture from those providers, according to the permissions you authorise on the provider's consent screen. We do not receive the password for those accounts.

3.3 One-time password verification (OTP)

To verify your phone or email we send temporary codes through SMS and transactional email providers. We process your phone number or email address for that purpose only.

3.4 Your communications

Messages, files and documents

The content of your messages is stored on our servers encrypted with AES-256 so that you can reach your history from several devices. Attachments (images, video, audio, documents) are stored with a cloud storage provider.

What this encryption does and does not mean

Message content is never stored in plain text in our database and never travels unencrypted over the network. However, the encryption keys are managed by Sigo App, not by your device. This is not end-to-end encryption: technically Sigo App can decrypt the content of a message, and will do so only in the cases set out in section 8 (a request from a competent authority, or the investigation of a reported abuse). We say this plainly so that you can decide, with full information, what you share over this channel.

Voice calls, video calls and Walkie-Talkie mode

These communications are transmitted over WebRTC with encryption in transit (the standard DTLS-SRTP protocol): audio and video travel encrypted at all times between your device and the other participants. We do not record or store the content of calls. We keep only metadata: participants, date, time, duration and status (answered, missed, declined).

Messages with scheduled deletion

If you set a message to self-destruct, an automated process removes it from our servers within the time you specify.

3.5 Contacts

Sigo App does not access your phone's contact list. Your contact list inside Sigo is built only from the people you add within the app itself, by searching for a username, scanning a QR code, or using the shake-to-discover feature.

Shake-to-discover. It uses your device's accelerometer to detect the shaking gesture and, at that moment, look for other users making the same gesture. It does not use your geographic location and only runs while that screen is open.

3.6 Location

We access your location only when you authorise it and turn on a feature that needs it:

  • Send a one-off location in a chat: a single coordinate is transmitted at that moment.
  • Live location: while it is on, the app keeps a foreground service running (you will see a persistent notification) and periodically sends your coordinates to our servers to relay them to the chat participants you chose. You can stop it at any time; it also expires automatically when the period you set runs out, and an automated process then deletes the associated location records.

You can revoke the location permission in your operating system settings at any time.

3.7 Camera, microphone, gallery and biometrics

  • Camera and microphone: to take photos and videos, record voice notes, make calls and scan QR codes.
  • Gallery and files: to attach content you select. We only access the files you choose.
  • Biometrics (fingerprint or face): if you turn on biometric lock, the verification is performed entirely by your device's operating system. Sigo App never receives, sees or stores your biometric data; it only receives a "verification successful" confirmation.

3.8 Integration with external email accounts

Sigo App lets you optionally connect a Gmail (Google) or Outlook (Microsoft) account to read and send email from within the app.

If you turn this feature on and give your consent on the provider's screen:

  • Permissions we request: reading your messages (gmail.readonly or the Microsoft Graph equivalent) and sending messages on your behalf (gmail.send or equivalent).
  • What we store on our servers: the message identifier, thread, sender, recipients (including CC and BCC), subject, date, read status, folder, and the message body, so that we can show you your inbox inside Sigo without depending on a permanent connection to the provider.
  • Access tokens: OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored encrypted with AES-256 using a random initialisation vector.
  • What we use it for: solely to provide the email feature inside Sigo App — showing, searching, flagging, organising and sending your mail.
  • What we do NOT use it for: we do not analyse the content of your email for advertising, we do not build profiles from it, we do not use it to train artificial intelligence models, and we do not share it with third parties.
Compliance with the Google API Services User Data Policy

Sigo App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can disconnect your email account at any time from the Sigo App mail inbox, using the "Disconnect" button. When you do, we delete the tokens and the synchronised email from our servers. You can also revoke access directly at myaccount.google.com/permissions or in the Microsoft account portal.

3.10 Business channels

If you create a business channel, we also process the commercial information you publish: channel identity, product catalogue, posts, external links, business contact details and automated replies. We also record reactions and views of posts, in aggregate form, to give you channel statistics.

3.11 Technical and usage data

  • IP address
  • Device model, operating system and app version
  • Session identifier and push notification token
  • Error logs and server activity logs
  • Connection metadata needed to keep real-time sessions alive

3.12 Data we do NOT collect

To be clear, Sigo App does not collect: your phone's contact list, the list of apps installed on your device, your web browsing history, your biometric data, your health data, or advertising identifiers for cross-app tracking.

5. International transfers

Our servers and those of several of our providers are located outside your country of residence, in particular in the United States and the European Union. By using Sigo App, your data may be transferred to and processed in those jurisdictions.

These transfers are covered by Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission, or by the applicable adequacy frameworks. You can request a copy of the safeguards in place by writing to the address in section 16.

6. How we protect your data

  • Encryption in transit: all traffic between the app and our servers travels over TLS. The app does not accept unencrypted connections.
  • Encryption at rest: message content and email account tokens are stored encrypted with AES-256.
  • Encryption in transit (WebRTC/DTLS-SRTP) on voice calls, video calls and Walkie-Talkie: audio and video travel encrypted at all times.
  • Passwords stored hashed and salted; never in reversible form.
  • On your device: session tokens and credentials are stored encrypted through the operating system's keystore, and are excluded from cloud backups.

No system is infallible. If a security breach occurs that may pose a risk to your rights, we will notify you without undue delay and, at the latest, within 72 hours of becoming aware of it, and we will report it to the competent supervisory authority.

8. When we share your data

We share your data only in these cases:

  1. With other users, according to the interactions you start: the recipients of your messages see your name, photo and the content you send them; channel members see what you publish there.
  2. With the providers listed in section 4, to the extent necessary to deliver each feature.
  3. With competent authorities, where there is a valid and binding judicial or administrative request. We verify the legitimacy of each request and limit it to what is strictly asked for. Where the law allows us to, we will let you know before handing over your data.
  4. To protect rights, where necessary to investigate fraud, a reported abuse or a threat to the safety of people or of the service.
  5. In a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, informing you in advance and maintaining the safeguards in this Policy.

We do not sell your personal data to anyone, under any circumstances.

9. User types and permissions

Sigo App distinguishes between personal users and business users, and applies a role-based access control model that determines what actions each user can take, what information they can see and what features are available depending on their account type.

10. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we process about you.
  • Rectify inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erase your data (the "right to be forgotten").
  • Restrict processing in certain circumstances.
  • Object to processing based on our legitimate interest.
  • Portability: receive your data in a structured, commonly used format (JSON), and ask for it to be transmitted to another controller.
  • Withdraw your consent at any time, for the features that rely on it.
  • Not be subject to automated decisions with significant legal effects. Sigo App does not make decisions of this kind.
  • Lodge a complaint with the data protection supervisory authority in your country.

To exercise any of these rights, write to us at the address in section 16. We respond within a maximum of thirty (30) calendar days. We may ask you to prove your identity before acting on the request.

Exercising these rights is free of charge, except for manifestly unfounded or excessive requests.

11. Deleting your account

You can request deletion of your account at any time:

  • From the app: Discover tab → Me → Delete account.
  • From the web, without needing the app installed: account deletion page.
  • By email: writing to sigo_soporte@gmail.com.

11.1 What happens when you request it

Immediately. Your account is deactivated. We close all your sessions, you stop receiving notifications, and you can no longer sign in — not with a password, not with your fingerprint, and not with Google or Facebook. We also disconnect any external email accounts you had linked and stop synchronising your inbox.

For the next 30 days. The account stays in a recovery period. If you change your mind, write to us from the email or phone number you registered with and we will reactivate it. During this period, the people you were already talking to still see your name and photo in those conversations: removing them earlier would make recovery impossible.

After 30 days. An automated process carries out the final deletion, which cannot be reversed.

11.2 What is deleted, what is anonymised and what is kept

We are precise about this, because not everything is treated the same way.

Deleted entirely Anonymised Kept
Your conversations with the Täbu assistant and all their messages Your first and last names, which are replaced by "Deleted account" The messages and files you sent to other people, which remain in those recipients' conversations
Linked email accounts, synchronised email and their access tokens Your identity document number Call and Walkie-Talkie history, with its metadata (participants, date, duration), subject to the general 12-month period in section 7
Your contact list within Sigo Your username, email address and phone number Data we must retain by legal obligation
Your profile picture, including the copy held in cloud storage Your postal address and date of birth —
Your active sessions, pending verification codes and your device's notification identifier Your password, your Google and Facebook identifiers, and your QR code —
Why we anonymise instead of deleting your record

Your messages are not only yours: they live inside conversations shared with other people. Deleting your record entirely would leave those conversations broken for everyone who talked to you. So we keep the record, but emptied of all personal data. From the final deletion onwards, anywhere your name used to appear, other people see "Deleted account".

Microsoft email accounts. When we disconnect a linked account we delete the access tokens from our servers and can no longer read your mail. For Google, we also revoke the permission automatically. Microsoft does not offer an equivalent way to do this from our side, so if you linked an Outlook account we recommend withdrawing the permission yourself from your Microsoft account privacy settings.

Backups. After the final deletion, data may persist for up to 30 additional days in encrypted backups before being overwritten.
